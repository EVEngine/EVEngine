详细描述

Own a complete Pcg-style terrain grid and its height, splat, detail, tree, object and probe state. Creation and every operation publish by one owner swap. History snapshots contain every domain, so undo/redo cannot expose a height/resource combination that never existed. Inputs are copied during calls and never retained. The workspace is caller-thread affine, invokes no callback, reads no clock and uses only explicitly supplied seeds.
