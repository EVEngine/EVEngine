详细描述

ITaskService adapter for asynchronous move-to requests.

备注
The adapter uniquely owns its provider and request factory. Active tickets are keyed by agent, state and task identity. Destruction and stop abandon every outstanding ticket, so provider results cannot target a later task incarnation.
