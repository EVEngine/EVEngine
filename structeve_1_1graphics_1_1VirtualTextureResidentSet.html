详细描述

CPU images and sampling metadata for one atomically publishable physical cache.

Slot zero contains a coarse whole-surface substitute. Resident virtual pages occupy the remaining slots; page-table blue is one for a resident page and zero for the substitute. Red/green contain normalized physical-slot centres. Upload all three images before publishing the associated material state.
