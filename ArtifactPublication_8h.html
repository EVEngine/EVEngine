详细描述

Low-level capability contracts for publishing backend-neutral artifacts.

This header intentionally contains no procgen, scene, graphics, map or physics include. Procgen owns the producer-side conversion from its typed GeneratedArtifact into this short-lived view; consumer modules own the providers and their runtime registries.
