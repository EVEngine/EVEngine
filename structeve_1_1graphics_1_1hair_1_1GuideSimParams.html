详细描述

Parameters for CPU guide-strand dynamics (UE groom sim lite analogue).

Verlet integration + XPBD distance constraints along each guide. Roots are pinned. No dependency on the physics module — a SoftBody3D bridge can be added later via capability without upward includes.
