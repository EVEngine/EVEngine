b2Fixture * eve::physics::Fixture::raw ( )
inline

Exposes the underlying Box2D fixture for tightly-scoped backend integration.

返回
Borrowed nullable backend pointer; callers must not delete or retain it across steps. @ownership The Box2D world owns the fixture; Fixture is only its wrapper. @lifetime Valid until Fixture::destroy(), body/world destruction, or invalidate(). @thread Call only on the owning physics thread. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks and is invalid across world mutation.
