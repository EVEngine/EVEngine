详细描述

TVE secondary material layer evaluated after primary texture sampling. Three borrowed bindings contain secondary albedo, normal and mask data. UV mode 0 uses the primary mesh UV, 1 uses the authored TVE detail UV and 2 projects world XZ. All scalar masks are remapped with the TVE 0.0001 epsilon; reversed endpoints are valid.
