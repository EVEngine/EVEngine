Apply one Pcg underwater audio transition to caller-owned sources.

参数
submergeDownOptional one-shot source played when playDown is true.
submergeUpOptional one-shot source played when playUp is true.
ambienceRequired looping underwater ambience source.
playDownWhether to play the downward transition once.
playUpWhether to play the upward transition once.
loopAmbienceWhether ambience should be playing after this call.
volumeNormalized transition and ambience gain in [0,1].
返回
Success or InvalidArgument; validation completes before source mutation. @ownership Sources remain caller-owned and are never retained. @thread Audio owner thread only; callbacks are not invoked.
