详细描述

Owner-thread script façade. Domain rules live on ActionRuntime / AbilityRuntime / AbilityAsset.

@ownership Unique; transferred into a Squirrel instance by newRuntime(). @thread Owner/main thread only; not reentrant from callbacks.
