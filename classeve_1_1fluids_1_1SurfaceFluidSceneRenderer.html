详细描述

Draws surface-droplet cap instances through the engine 3D backend.

The renderer is backend-neutral at its public boundary and uses Graphics' sphere mesh + PBR path. On Vulkan this writes regular scene depth and receives environment lighting; WebGPU consumes the same transforms as a fallback.
