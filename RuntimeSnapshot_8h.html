详细描述

Shared SnapshotEnvelope codec for typed definition runtime instances.

The codec owns only the stable identity/instance-state envelope shape. A domain supplies an encoder and decoder for its typed state. Definitions are intentionally referenced, not copied into this common protocol: the registry remains the sole owner of definition data.
