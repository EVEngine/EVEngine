详细描述

Independently owned, pooled visual effect; main/render thread only, no callbacks or gameplay damage. Graphics resources and an internal generation-checked render entity are retired in either destruction order. Endpoint positions are copied; no weapon pointer is retained. Recreate after reload/provider retirement.
