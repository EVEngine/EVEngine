详细描述

Minimal observable contract for physics simulation providers.

The backend borrows domain-owned state and must be destroyed before that state. Implementations own no graphics or presentation resources. Calls are simulation-thread affine and must not run concurrently with another mutation of the same domain state. A failed step leaves observation and domain state unchanged.
