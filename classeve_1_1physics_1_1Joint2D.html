详细描述

Owning planar joint wrapper observing World-owned backend state. @ownership Caller/VM owns factory wrappers; World owns restored wrappers and all solver joints. @lifetime Body/world/source-joint destruction or restore invalidates observers; never retain a raw backend pointer. @thread All methods and destruction are simulation-owner-thread only, outside solver callbacks/step. @reentrancy No callbacks; no locks or reentrant world mutation. Inputs are borrowed only during calls.

Gear source identities use generation handles. Source-first destroys dependent gears; gear-first leaves sources intact. No state is reconstructed from pointer addresses.
