详细描述

Stateful revision cursor that projects one PixelWorld interest region.

The cursor owns only consumer projection state: source link, last revision and known Chunk coordinates. PixelWorld remains the sole material authority. APIs are simulation-thread affine, retain no world pointer and invoke no callbacks.
