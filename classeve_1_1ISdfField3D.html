详细描述

Borrowed 3D signed-distance field for cross-module collision queries.

Negative samples are inside the solid. Providers live in higher modules (physics, fluids, gameplay) and register through capability wiring; consumers only sample this interface and never include provider headers.

@ownership Borrowed for the duration of the consumer binding. Callers must clear the binding before destroying the field. @thread Owner thread only; not reentrant from unknown callbacks.
