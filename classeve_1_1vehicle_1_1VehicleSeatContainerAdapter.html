详细描述

Transactional adapter over one VehicleEntity::Seats component.

The ECS entity is held by a generation-qualified handle. The adapter does not own the vehicle or occupants. A prepared state must be committed or rolled back before the vehicle entity is destroyed; stale destruction is rejected during snapshot/prepare and an invalid lifetime during noexcept commit terminates, as required by IContainer::PreparedState.

Seat membership is explicit-slot, capacity equals the number of declared seats, and the seat component's revision is the sole optimistic-concurrency revision. VehicleSystem's legacy enter/exit facade delegates to this class.
