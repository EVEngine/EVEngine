详细描述

Finite-duration pose transition relative to a continuously sampled target.

Owns all history and output; retains no skeleton, clip or input pose pointers. Local TRS inputs must share bone order and coordinate space. Unlike ControlPose, this converges exactly at the requested deadline and does not continually lag its target. @thread Owner thread only; no callbacks or reentrancy. Explicit elapsed simulation time makes repeated evaluation deterministic within floating-point tolerance.
