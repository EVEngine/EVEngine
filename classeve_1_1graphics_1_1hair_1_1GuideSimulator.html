详细描述

Lightweight Verlet + XPBD simulator for guide curves only.

Owns mutable particle state derived from a rest StrandsDatas. Call snapshot() to obtain a deformed guides buffer for interpolateStrands.

@thread Affine to the caller; not synchronized. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks. @determinism Tolerance-bounded for identical params/seeded inputs.
