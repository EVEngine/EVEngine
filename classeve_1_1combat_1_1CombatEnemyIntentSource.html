详细描述

Deterministic enemy intent source for arena vertical slices.

When the locked target is inside the near band and the cooldown elapsed, the brain enters Telegraph (if authored), then emits one light-attack intent, then Recover. Telegraph and Recover are punishable. Zero telegraph/recover ticks keep the original immediate-attack contract.
