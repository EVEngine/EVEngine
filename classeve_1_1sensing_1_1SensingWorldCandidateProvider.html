详细描述

ISensingCandidateProvider adapter over a SensingWorld fact mirror.

Supports World2D queries only. Grid constraints remain Unsupported. Zone filters use mirrored subject zone membership (SensingWorld::setZones). Domain filters require an injected FactionRelationFn.

@ownership Non-owning pointer to SensingWorld; the world must outlive this provider. @thread Call on the same simulation thread as the bound SensingWorld.
