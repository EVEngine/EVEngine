详细描述

Owning command log and periodic checkpoint projection for PixelWorld.

The log is not another mutable world authority. It owns immutable replay inputs and digests only. APIs are simulation-thread affine, invoke no callbacks, and use bit-exact integer hashes over canonical PixelWorld bytes.
