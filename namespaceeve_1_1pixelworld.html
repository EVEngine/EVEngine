Generate a seamless canonical Chunk batch without mutating a PixelWorld.

参数
requestVersioned finite generation request and optional owning stamps.
catalogCatalog whose ids/default cells give generated materials meaning.
sourceRevisionPositive authority revision assigned to every output Chunk.
sourceTickAuthority Tick carried by the correction batch.
sourceLastEditSequenceAuthority edit sequence carried by the correction batch.
返回
Owning canonical batch and summaries, or a structured bounded-input error.
备注
Integer coordinate hashing makes output bit-exact across worker count and backend. The caller may transactionally admit output.batch through PixelWorld::applyChunkBatch. Unknown schema versions are rejected and no world state is observed or changed.
