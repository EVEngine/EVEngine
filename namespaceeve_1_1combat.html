◆ ActionCombatStateResolver

using eve::combat::ActionCombatStateResolver = typedef std::function<OptionalRef<CombatState>(ecs::EntityHandle)>

Resolves a borrowed mutable CombatState for one live ECS handle.

备注
Called synchronously on the owner thread without an engine lock. The returned reference must remain valid through the current dispatch, repeated resolution of the same handle must be stable during that dispatch, and the callback must not throw or reenter the sink.

◆ standardCombatAbilities()

EVENGINE_API_BACKENDS Result< std::vector< action::AbilityDefinition > > eve::combat::standardCombatAbilities ( )

Build twenty validated, registerable standard combat ability definitions.

The catalogue supplies identities, timings, cooldown/instancing/group policy and adapter metadata. Projects attach animation, movement, damage and other presentation/gameplay behavior through Action timeline events and executors.

返回
Owning definitions, or a structured internal-definition failure.
