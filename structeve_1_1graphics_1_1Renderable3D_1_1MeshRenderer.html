Shader* eve::graphics::Renderable3D::MeshRenderer::xrayShader = nullptr

Optional X-ray shader used to paint this entity's occluded silhouette (see Shader::setXray). When xrayHighlight is set, the entity is skipped from the G-buffer (so its pixels record occluder depth) and drawn a second time over the scene with the X-ray shader.
