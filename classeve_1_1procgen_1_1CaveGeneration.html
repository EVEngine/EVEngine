详细描述

Staged limestone generator sharing the canonical mesh.cave algorithms.

备注
Owns parameters, skeleton and dense intermediate fields. No borrowed library, VM callback, graphics or ECS state survives a call. All failed operations preserve the prior stage. Stage values are transient; persist the script URI, configuration and seed and rebuild on reload. Within one build/compiler the legacy schedule is bit-exact; cross-platform floats have no bitwise promise. @thread Owner thread only; no callbacks, locks or reentrant execution.
