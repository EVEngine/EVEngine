详细描述

Declarative authoring composition: hierarchy plus source and presentation payloads. Owns authored values; meshes, imported hosts and cameras are rebuildable projections. @thread Owner thread only, no callbacks. No native pointers are persisted.

备注
Schema eve.scene.authoring version 4 migrates v1/v2/v3 and rejects unknown fields and versions. Source references describe immutable asset revisions; importing does not overwrite authored TRS.
