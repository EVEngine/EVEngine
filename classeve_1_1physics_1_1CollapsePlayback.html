详细描述

Independent clock over a frozen, shared collapse animation. @ownership Owns a shared immutable snapshot. Copies share samples but copy clock state. @lifetime No world, renderer or caller-owned pointers are retained. @thread Each instance requires exclusive access during mutation; separate copies may run concurrently. No callbacks, IO, wall clock or physical simulation occur during playback.

◆ prepare()

Result< CollapsePlayback > eve::physics::CollapsePlayback::prepare ( CollapseAnimation  animation)
static

Freeze an owning clip, rejecting empty recordings.

参数
animationValue snapshot; move an existing clip to avoid copying its samples. @cost Copying an lvalue is linear in recorded samples; prepare once and copy players cheaply.
返回
Player at zero seconds, running at rate one, with clamped endpoints, or an internal allocation failure diagnostic. Existing players are unchanged; a moved input is consumed even on failure.
异常
std::bad_allocIf copying the by-value argument or allocating the failure diagnostic fails.
