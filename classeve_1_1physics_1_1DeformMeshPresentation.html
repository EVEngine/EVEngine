详细描述

Prepared dynamic mesh presentation over an owned deformation player. @ownership Owns immutable asset and CPU buffers only; the caller owns playback state. Never retains Graphics, Mesh, ECS or source simulation pointers. @thread Render-owner thread, exclusive use; no callbacks. Caller owns graphics-resource lifecycle.
