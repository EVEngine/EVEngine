详细描述

Render-thread-affine RGBA atlas projection of a rectangular PixelWorld region. @ownership The renderer owns only its CPU atlas; Graphics owns the Texture handle. @lifetime A renderer using a texture must be destroyed before its Graphics backend.

The renderer never owns or retains the PixelWorld/Graphics arguments supplied to sync(). Its Texture is a borrowed backend-owned handle and must not outlive Graphics. The CPU atlas is authoritative only for presentation and is rebuilt after world revision rollback (clear/restore); gameplay continues to read PixelWorld itself.
