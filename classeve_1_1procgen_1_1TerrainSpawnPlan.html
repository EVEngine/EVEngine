详细描述

Own an ordered mixed-domain Pcg-style spawn recipe. Rule IDs are stable and unique. Every raster and setting is copied at insertion, so later caller mutation cannot alter replay. The plan is a value object with no scene references, callbacks, clock or mutable external state.
