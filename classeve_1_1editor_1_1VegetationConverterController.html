详细描述

UI-neutral TVE converter controller with revision-safe prepare and publish phases. @ownership The preparer and publisher are borrowed and must outlive this controller. @thread Owner thread only. Calls are synchronous and no controller lock is held across either boundary. @reentrancy Preparer and publisher implementations must not call this controller recursively.
