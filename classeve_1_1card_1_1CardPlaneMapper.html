eve::Result< void > eve::card::CardPlaneMapper::mapCardPose ( float  x,
float  y,
float  layoutAngleDeg,
float  flipProgress,
float  liftPeak,
int  flipAxis 
)

Map a card's logical center into a lifted/flipped world pose on the plane.

参数
xLogical center X.
yLogical center Y.
layoutAngleDegIn-plane rotation (Hand fan angle), degrees.
flipProgressAnimated face-down amount in [0, 1].
liftPeakPeak rise along the plane normal at the edge-on midpoint.
flipAxis0 = rotate about local up (width foreshortens), 1 = about local right.
返回
Success with cached world position and orthonormal basis U/V/N, or InvalidArgument when the logical rect or plane axes degenerate.
备注
Synchronous owner-thread query; does not allocate. Read getters after success.
