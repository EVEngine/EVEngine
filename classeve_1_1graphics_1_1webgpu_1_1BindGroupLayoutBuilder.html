详细描述

Fluent builder for wgpu::BindGroupLayout.

Builds the C++ wrapper descriptor directly (whose zero-init already matches the WebGPU defaults) instead of a hand-written WGPUBindGroupLayoutEntry array

  • reinterpret_cast. Mirrors vk-bootstrap's DescriptorSetBuilder.
