详细描述

Workspace controller for one authored biome rules document.

Owns the document, local undo, borrowed spatial domains and a copied PointSet preview. Preview requests always carry the published revision; a stale or failed generation keeps the last successful points on screen.

@ownership Editor owns the document and spatial copies. Preview getters copy values. @threadaffinity Owner thread only. @reentrancy No unknown callbacks.
