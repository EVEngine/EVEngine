详细描述

Read-only state boundary used by every condition evaluation.

Implementations must not mutate domain state. Methods are synchronous and called on the caller's thread; implementations must not retain the caller's query keys or invoke unknown callbacks while holding a lock. nullopt means the requested capability/value is unavailable, which is distinct from a known false value.
