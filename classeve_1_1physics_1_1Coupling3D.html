详细描述

Owning 3D transmission constraint, stepped transactionally by its World3D. @ownership Caller/VM owns wrapper; World registers an observer and invalidates it before teardown. @lifetime Body/source-joint/world destruction invalidates the wrapper; no backend pointers persist across steps. @thread All operations and destruction require the simulation owner thread, outside callbacks/World3Dstep. @reentrancy No callbacks or locks. Source references are borrowed only for construction.

Explicit velocity projection precedes the native solver; positional drift uses bounded bias correction. Revolute coordinates unwrap across turns and require less than pi radians of relative rotation per step. This is a finite-step transmission model; it does not simulate tooth contact or promise bit-exact replay.
