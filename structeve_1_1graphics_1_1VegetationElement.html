详细描述

Owning element definition; no scene, texture or entity pointers survive admission. Center/extents are world units; yaw is radians around +Y. Layer bits select 0..8. Mask alpha multiplies opacity and mask RGB multiplies the target RGB. Equal priorities preserve input order. Seasons interpolate winter/spring/summer/autumn.
