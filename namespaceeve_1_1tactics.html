◆ TurnOrder

enum class eve::tactics::TurnOrder : std::uint8_t
strong

The total order a policy reports for one pair of units.

This is a named three-valued result rather than a bool on purpose: "equivalent" is a real outcome with real meaning (the caller then applies the canonical subject tie-break), and a bool cannot express it without an out-of-band convention that every reader has to know.

枚举值
LeftFirst 

The left unit acts first.

RightFirst 

The right unit acts first.

Equivalent 

Both compare equal; the caller decides by canonical order.
