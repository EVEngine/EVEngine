详细描述

Independent clock and placement over a shared immutable deformation clip. @ownership Owns reusable scratch storage; shares only immutable clip samples. No source/world pointers. @thread Each player, including sampling, requires exclusive access; separate players are independent.

备注
Move-only to make buffer preparation explicit. Moved-from objects may only be destroyed or reassigned.
