详细描述

World-space analytic light copied during scene preparation.

Point strength is RGB radiant intensity with inverse-square falloff. Directional strength is RGB irradiance perpendicular to directionToLight. All colors are linear; directionToLight must be finite and nonzero for directional lights. Spot strength has the same units as point strength on the beam axis. The beam points away from the source; angular attenuation is the square of the clamped linear ramp between outer and inner cone cosines. Equal cosines form a hard rim.
