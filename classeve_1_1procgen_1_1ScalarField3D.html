详细描述

Owning regular scalar volume; x is contiguous, followed by y and z.

备注
Samples use the caller's coordinate system. Cave volumes span [-1,1] on each axis; nonnegative density is rock. Copies are independent. No ECS, VM or graphics resources are retained. @thread One owner thread for mutation; independent copies may be processed on other threads.
