详细描述

Incremental static collision projection of authoritative PixelWorld chunks.

The cache owns only process-local PhysicsLinks and revision metadata. Physics World owns all bodies/fixtures and PixelWorld owns all material cells. Sync is owner-thread affine, invokes no callbacks, and stages every replacement body before committing.
