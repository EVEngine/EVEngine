详细描述

Builds renderer-independent droplet instances and wet PBR parameters.

Major/minor axes lie in the current surface tangent plane. Their footprint preserves area while velocity changes aspect ratio, avoiding billboard-like circles and keeping the data usable by Vulkan, WebGPU and CPU references.
