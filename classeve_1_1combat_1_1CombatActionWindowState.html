详细描述

Authoritative combat projection of active Montage hitbox and invulnerability windows.

Keys combine ActionExecutionId and timeline item identity, so overlapping windows compose without a lossy boolean. Exit uses the retained owning SubjectRef and is safe when the ECS entity was destroyed after enter. This object is owner-thread-only.
