◆ DeterminismLevel

enum class eve::decision::DeterminismLevel : std::uint8_t
strong

Determinism contract declared by a script-backed condition policy.

枚举值
BitExact 

Same inputs produce the same bits without an external clock.

TickDeterministic 

Deterministic when evaluated at the injected simulation tick.

ToleranceBounded 

Results may vary within a documented numeric tolerance.

ExplicitlyNondeterministic 

Result is intentionally not suitable for replay or lockstep.
