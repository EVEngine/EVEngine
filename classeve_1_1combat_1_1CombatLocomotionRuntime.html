详细描述

Owner-thread combat locomotion authority shared by player and navigation commands.

The runtime owns every mutable movement fact. Navigation is optional and borrowed; advance prepares all states before one publication, so a provider failure cannot leave a partially moved arena. No method reads a clock or RNG.
