详细描述

One-draw particle shadow caster matching Fluid3D ShadowmapExposer behavior. @ownership Borrows Graphics for its lifetime and owns one Graphics-managed dynamic mesh. @thread Construction, update and destruction are render-thread affine. @reentrancy Invokes no script callbacks; destruction must occur outside shadow traversal.
