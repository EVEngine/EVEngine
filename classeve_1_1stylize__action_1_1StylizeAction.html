详细描述

Composition entry for AttackVfx Action notify handlers and layer bridges.

@ownership Owns the module-scoped AttackVfxRuntime pool. Layer GPU/audio resources remain with registered IAttackVfxLayerExecutor providers. @thread Owner-thread only.
