Split every source triangle against a regular X/Z grid like GTSMeshSplitter.

参数
sourceImmutable triangle mesh with aligned position, normal and UV streams.
xSplitsNumber of vertical split planes; output columns equal xSplits plus one.
zSplitsNumber of horizontal split planes; output rows equal zSplits plus one.
pivotOptional per-cell vertex translation with a matching restoration offset.
返回
Owned row-major cells; empty cells are retained.
