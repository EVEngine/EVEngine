详细描述

Fluent builder for wgpu::RenderPipeline.

Builds the C++ wrapper RenderPipelineDescriptor directly, whose zero-init already matches the WebGPU defaults (writeMask=All, sampleMask=0xFFFFFFFF), so the "default discards every fragment" traps in Graphics.cpp cannot occur. Mirrors vk-bootstrap's PipelineBuilder.
