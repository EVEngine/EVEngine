详细描述

Human-readable, scoped identifier in the form namespace:name.

LogicalId is not a persistent instance identity. It names a definition, recipe, policy or action and may be reused by many runtime instances. The namespace is canonical lower-case ASCII ([a-z][a-z0-9_.-]*); the name is case-sensitive ASCII ([A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*). This prevents modules from inventing incompatible namespace spellings at a persistence boundary.
