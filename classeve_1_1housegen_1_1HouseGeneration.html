详细描述

Editable floor-by-floor house generation with an owning component-library snapshot.

备注
The session owns all intermediate data. No library references, VM objects or ECS identities are retained. Failed configuration, floor emission or validation preserves the prior session. Defaults preserve the legacy RNG schedule within one build/compiler. Persist configuration and seed, not this transient session; rebuild on hot reload. No cross-platform bitwise float guarantee is made. @thread Owner thread only; synchronous, no callbacks or reentrant calls.
