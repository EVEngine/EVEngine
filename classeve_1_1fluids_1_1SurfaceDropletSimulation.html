详细描述

CPU reference solver for droplets moving over static, rigid or deforming surfaces.

The binding owns topology and poses; this solver owns only material-space droplet addresses. It applies gravity relative to surface acceleration, transports droplets across triangle adjacency, and emits world-space states at open edges or when the outward acceleration exceeds adhesion.
