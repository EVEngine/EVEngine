详细描述

Owning CPU sprite appearance, with pixel rectangle, degree rotation, color and normalized atlas UV.

备注
Contains no texture, shader, camera, ECS or simulation references. Rectangle placement follows the live renderer, including its existing stretched-sprite anchor. Resource selection remains with the presenter.
