详细描述

SLG / large-map war-fog overlay driven by a dual-scrolling cloud texture and an RGBA mask bridge (R unlock, G select, B dissolve).

Presentation only: gameplay unlock state should live in map::Fov / game data; upload that state into the mask R channel and call draw() after the map.
