详细描述

Owning detached bitmap plus a non-owning, stale-safe dynamic body link.

PixelWorld remains the only owner of live terrain. This adapter owns the detached bitmap while Physics World owns the body and fixtures. It stores no World/Body raw pointer across calls; every operation resolves PhysicsLink against a caller-borrowed world on the owning simulation thread. No method invokes scripts or unknown callbacks.
