详细描述

Move-only, single-use collection of validated force requests. @ownership Owns values and handles only, never world/body pointers. Safe to discard. @thread Prepare/commit on the world's simulation owner, outside solver callbacks. @reentrancy No callbacks. A successful commit consumes the batch; failure changes no bodies.

Commit revalidates all targets and recomputes moment arms at current centers of mass. Binds forces to an exact tick/duration. A failed step preserves pending forces for retry without resubmission. All batches pending in a world must name the same step. Direct raw stepping/force clearing is forbidden. Do not mix unbounded raw-backend force accumulation with this API.
