详细描述

Value configuration for a rectangular stamp in world X/Z coordinates.

Terrain sample (x,y) is at (originX+x*spacingX, originZ+y*spacingZ). Stamp width/depth span its first and last texel. Positive rotation is counterclockwise in the X/Z plane; radians. No time or randomness is used. Heights are finite, unbounded world heights, not normalized/packed values.
