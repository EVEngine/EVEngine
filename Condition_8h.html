详细描述

Pure, explainable condition trees shared by gameplay domains.

The AST is intentionally thin: boolean combinators, Compare over a keyed value space, and PolicyCall for named read-only policies. Tag / attribute / resource / state / authority checks are key and policy conventions documented in ConditionKeys.h — they are not ConditionKind variants.
