◆ RoadJunctionControl

enum class eve::procgen::road::RoadJunctionControl : std::uint8_t
strong

Authoritative traffic-control policy applied to every approach of one junction node.

枚举值
Uncontrolled 

Edge trafficPriority resolves right-of-way without a mandatory stop.

Yield 

Lower-priority approaches yield; equal-priority approaches all yield.

Stop 

Every incoming approach receives a stop control.

Signal 

Every incoming approach is controlled by a traffic signal.
