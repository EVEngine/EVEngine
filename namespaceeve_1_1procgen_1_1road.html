◆ bakeRoadNetwork()

EVENGINE_API_DOMAINS Result< RoadBakeResult > eve::procgen::road::bakeRoadNetwork ( const RoadNetwork &  network,
const RoadBakeOptions &  options = {} 
)

Bake every edge, junction platform, pier, marking and navigation overlay.

参数
networkBorrowed live network; must outlive the call only.
optionsBake knobs; sample counts are bounded to [2,4096], and enabled navigation dimensions must be finite and positive.
返回
Owning mesh/overlay snapshot, or a structured diagnostic. @cost Geometry generation grows with output; bounded parameter scans may dominate sparse outputs, and each probe scales with path point count. Prefab weld/smoothing adds logarithmic grouping per vertex. Probe work and output are bounded by maximumSamplingEvaluations, maximumMeshElements and maximumPlacements, amortized at explicit bake boundaries. Terrain projection adds one provider call per center anchor or samplesPerAxis squared calls per footprint, doubled for distinct projected top/bottom layers of tilted assets. TerrainNormal alignment adds one center-normal query per candidate anchor. All height and normal queries share maximumPlacementQueries across the network. Retained road frames are owned once per edge for this bake; SurfaceSegments placement shares their scan budget, prepares linear cumulative metrics and uses logarithmic row lookup per anchor. Neighbor clearance scans retained visible segments linearly. Peak frame storage grows with total retained rows, with up to two socket rows per edge. Analytic Fixed/Average placement uses 512 arc probes per span and up to 4096 orientation frames per anchor; this costs more than historical 32-probe LegacyCentered placement and is paid only at explicit bake boundaries.
注解
