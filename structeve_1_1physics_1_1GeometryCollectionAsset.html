详细描述

Immutable cooked/authored geometry-collection asset.

Version 2 adds clusterId/fractureLevel on bones. Version 1 documents migrate in fromValue (clusterId=0, fractureLevel=0). Unknown fields and unsupported versions are rejected; there is no silent downgrade.
