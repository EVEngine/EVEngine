详细描述

A data-flow graph for attributed procedural points.

Nodes are addressed by stable string ids and evaluated lazily from a chosen output. Graph mutations invalidate cached results. Execution rejects missing inputs and cycles, retains inspectable node outputs, and supports nested graphs through the subgraph operation.
