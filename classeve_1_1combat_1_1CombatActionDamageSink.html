详细描述

Explicitly registered bridge from Action damage blocks to DamageRuntime.

The game owns entity-to-CombatState resolution and both states. This adapter owns only its resolver, deterministic DamageRuntime, and the latest owning outcome. All methods are owner-thread-only; destruction automatically unregisters the sink.
