详细描述

Timeline-style controller for one authored audio source.

Owns the document, local undo, waveform cache and audition transport. PCM identity is separate from document revision so volume/pitch edits do not stale-unbind playback. Clip PCM changes increment pcmRevision.

@ownership Editor owns the document and clock backend. Live Source is borrowed from the Audio module when attachLiveAudition succeeds. @threadaffinity Owner thread only. @reentrancy No unknown callbacks.
