详细描述

Timeline editing controller shared by developer and in-game editors.

It owns selection, clipboard, preview transport and local undo history, but never a second timeline. Every edit creates an invertible DomainOperation and commits through the same authority/transaction path as other editors. All methods are owner-thread-only and invoke no external callbacks.
