详细描述

Optional damage rule that multiplies health damage by a body-part key.

The damage request's damageType suffix after the final '.' may encode a part override, but the preferred path sets incomingDamageMultiplier before apply or uses evaluateForPart. Missing parts use multiplier 1.0.
