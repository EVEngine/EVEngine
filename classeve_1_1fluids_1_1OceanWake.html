详细描述

Caller-owned wave field with finite-depth gravity dispersion and absorbing boundaries. No graphics/physics pointers or internal clock. All mutations are owner-thread only; const reads may run concurrently only while the owner is not mutating. No callbacks.

Result< std::unique_ptr< OceanWakeStep > > eve::fluids::OceanWake::prepareStep ( double  dt,
double  endTime,
const OceanSpectrumFrame &  water 
) const

Prepare an owning candidate for one fixed step with exact per-mode dispersion omega² = g*k*tanh(k*depth).

参数
dtSeconds in (0,1/30]; endTime must equal time()+dt within 1e-8 seconds.
endTimeInjected simulation timestamp, not a wall clock.
waterBorrowed immutable start-of-step frame, alive for this call only. Foam coverage follows its horizontal particle velocity using midpoint backtracing and bounded bilinear sampling. Open boundaries discard outgoing coverage; this is a dissipative coverage approximation, not conserved fluid mass. No input is retained.
返回
Owning candidate, InvalidArgument, or spectral query diagnostic. Preparation never changes the live owner; discard the candidate to cancel. @cost O(N² log N) plus two surface queries per foam-grid cell; execute once per interaction region per fixed step. Empty foam skips transport queries.
