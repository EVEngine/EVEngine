详细描述

Move-only lifetime token for one independently registered accelerator provider.

Registration is main-thread/module-startup affine. The provider remains externally owned and must outlive this token and every backend it created. Destroying or resetting the token removes only this provider; other domain providers remain registered. A lower priority value is attempted first when multiple providers support the same domain. The legacy single-provider eve::cap::provide<IAcceleratorBackendProvider>() entry remains supported and is considered after independently registered providers.
