详细描述

Pure-2D sprite stack made from horizontal RGBA cross-sections.

Layers are drawn bottom-to-top through the normal 2D textured-quad path. Each higher layer is offset upward by thickness 2D units. Rotation is applied in the 2D plane; no Camera3D, depth buffer, G-buffer, mesh, or 3D pass is used.
