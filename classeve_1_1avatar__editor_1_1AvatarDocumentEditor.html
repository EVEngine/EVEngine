详细描述

Timeline-style controller for one authored avatar document.

Owns the document, local undo and a CPU composite of authored layer rects. Live AvatarInstance publication stays candidate-first and is skipped until a texture resolver is attached; failed publish keeps the last successful layout.

@ownership Editor owns the document. Preview getters copy values. @threadaffinity Owner thread only. @reentrancy No unknown callbacks.
