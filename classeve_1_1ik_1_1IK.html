详细描述

Inverse Kinematics module — wraps header-only sunxfancy/ik.hpp (FABRIK). Script: ik <- eve.IK();

Provides 2D/3D skeleton + solver factories. No overloads: use Skeleton2D / Skeleton3D and Solver2D / Solver3D explicitly.
