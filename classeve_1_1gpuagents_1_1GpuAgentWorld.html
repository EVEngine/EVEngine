详细描述

World-level registry for GPU agent simulations and environment semantics.

Analogous to a World Subsystem: owns obstacle/surface fields, registers named EffectBackends that stay caller-owned, and prepares per-tick EnvironmentSnapshot values for every registered simulation.

@ownership Backends are borrowed; the caller / script VM retains ownership for the full World registration lifetime. @lifetime World must not outlive registered backends; unregister before destroy.
