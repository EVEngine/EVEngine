详细描述

Active-phase effect/operation preparation port.

Effect IDs are resolved by the provider against the canonical effects registry/container. A Weapon adapter can use the same port for its Active fire operation while preserving weapon-specific state in the weapon module. targets is borrowed for this call only. The returned unique_ptr is the sole owner of staged external state until ActionRuntime commits or rolls it back.
