详细描述

Interactive 3D cloth — Verlet particles + distance constraints. Meter space, +Y up, grid laid out in the XZ plane with the top row pinned.

Supports self-collision (spatial-hash proximity), a dihedral fold-angle limit between adjacent triangles (avoids excessive creasing), and particle-vs-rigid-body collision when attached to a World3D. Script-owned, independent of the Box3D world.
