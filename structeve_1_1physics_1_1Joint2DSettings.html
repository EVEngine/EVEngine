详细描述

Validated-at-creation joint parameters; all lengths are meters, angles radians.

anchorA/B are body attachment points in world space; revolute/prismatic/wheel/weld use anchorA as their common world anchor. axis is normalized after validation. Distance length must be positive. Pulley ground anchors are fixed world points and ratio > 0. springHertz/dampingRatio configure distance/wheel/weld softness (zero Hertz is rigid).
