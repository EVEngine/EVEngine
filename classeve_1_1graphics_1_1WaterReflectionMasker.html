详细描述

Own a CPU snapshot of Pcg's planar-reflection mask and evaluate player movement against it.

setMask copies ImageData pixels. evaluate mutates only settings.enabled and the retained height-feature state when the sampled boolean changes; no image or terrain pointer is retained. @thread Game thread only.
