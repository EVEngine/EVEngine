详细描述

Own ordered Pcg-style PolyMask nodes and rasterize them into a caller-owned scalar grid. Nodes are value-copied and no terrain, texture, scene object, callback, clock, or RNG is retained.

Rasterize closed fill and ordered additive brush strokes atomically.

参数
targetExclusively borrowed finite destination; dimensions must match grid.
brushBorrowed finite scalar brush. Samples outside each node's square footprint are zero.
gridPositive finite sample spacing and finite world origin.
typeOpen draws strokes only; Closed first fills the polygon when at least three nodes exist.
返回
Changed sample count or InvalidArgument; failure leaves target unchanged.
异常
std::bad_allocTarget remains unchanged. @thread Synchronous exclusive target access; immutable brush and owned-node snapshot, no callbacks. Pcg 4.2.2 passes node strength to ApplyBrushStroke but its shader path does not bind or consume it; this implementation retains the field for asset fidelity and likewise does not scale the brush by it.
