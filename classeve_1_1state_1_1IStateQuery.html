详细描述

Read-only world-state boundary owned by consumers such as Dialogue.

Implementations are synchronous and must not retain query arguments or invoke unknown callbacks while holding a lock. nullopt means that this provider does not own the requested domain/key. A returned false is a known negative answer. The provider is borrowed by the caller and must outlive the query context; it is not retained by the capability registry after revoke.
