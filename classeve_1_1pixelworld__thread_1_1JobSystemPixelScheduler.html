详细描述

Synchronous PixelWorld candidate scheduler backed by engine JobSystem.

The adapter borrows JobSystem for its whole lifetime and never stops or owns it. parallelFor joins every submitted job before returning. Worker failure falls back to a deterministic serial overwrite of all index-owned result slots.
