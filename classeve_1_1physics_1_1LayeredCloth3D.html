详细描述

Owns multiple cloth sheets and advances their contacts synchronously. @ownership Owns every layer; no external runtime pointers are retained. @thread All operations run on the owning physics thread, between draw calls. @reentrancy No callbacks. No concurrent calls or layer updates during update(). Runtime-only state; no persistence or GPU parity is claimed. Replays use fixed dt and stable layer order, with a 1e-5 metre tolerance on the same CPU build.
