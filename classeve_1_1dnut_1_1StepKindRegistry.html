详细描述

Canonical step vocabulary owner and runtime handler router.

Descriptors are the single source of truth for a step type's payload fields: the compiler validates authored steps against them and authoring tools derive their field lists from them, so no second field table exists.

All methods are owner-thread-only. Handlers are invoked synchronously without locks and must not retain their arguments.
