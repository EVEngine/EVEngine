详细描述

Presentation-only montage player over an ActionTimeline.

ActionRuntime remains the sole gameplay lifecycle authority. This object owns all resolved clips and only keeps a derived presentation cursor. It is owner-thread-only, reads no wall clock, and invokes the optional receiver without holding a lock.
