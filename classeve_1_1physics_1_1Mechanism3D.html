详细描述

Composed mechanical assembly owned by a World3D.

Builds crank-slider, shaft, and ratchet mechanisms from Joint3D primitives. Ratchet one-way locking is applied automatically during World3D::step.

@ownership World3D owns the mechanism and the joints it creates. Borrowed Body3D inputs are never retained as caller ownership. @lifetime Valid until Mechanism3D::destroy(), World3D::destroy(), or any dependent body/joint invalidation. @thread Call on the owning physics thread. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks.
