详细描述

Authoritative squad membership, post occupancy, and reinforce spawn requests.

Owns only roster facts (who belongs where, which seats are empty, which spawn requests are pending). Does not own HP, navigation, production queues, or NPC behavior trees — games inject subject ids and fulfill spawn requests via their own Production / ISpawnProvider path.

@thread Simulation owner thread only. @reentrancy No callbacks; no graphics includes.
