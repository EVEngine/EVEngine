详细描述

Scoped action damage provider using RPG Vitals as the sole health authority. @ownership Host owns this nonmovable sink and the resolver's captured mapping. @lifetime Destroy the sink before captured owners; actor destruction is handle-checked. @thread Create, dispatch, poll and destroy on the simulation owner thread. @reentrancy Resolver calls are synchronous without locks and must not reenter.

备注
Registers the existing eve.action.damage-sink capability. Owns no actors, CombatState or persistent data. Supports hp damage; poise/knockback are explicitly Unsupported. The host must select exactly one damage provider per target.

Settle one timeline hit against authoritative hp and queue its committed receipt.

返回
Applied, preview NoOp, or a structured failure without a receipt or health mutation. @ownership Inputs are borrowed only for this call; queued receipts own their identities. @thread Simulation owner thread; no concurrent registry mutation. @reentrancy Does not call scripts; the resolver must obey its non-reentrant contract.
备注
Uses injected tick and no random stream. Hosts decide critical rolls upstream. Only a positive-to-zero transition reports killed; repeated hits on a corpse do not. Delivery deduplication belongs to the timeline producer, not execution ID (multi-hit is valid). @cost One RPG settlement including attribute staging, more expensive than a Vitals read; invoked once per authored hit, never as a per-frame health synchronization.
