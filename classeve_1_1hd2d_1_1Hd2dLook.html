详细描述

HD-2D presentation look inspired by HD2DURP (DOF + bloom + pixel sampling).

Applies camera post settings for the miniature look and nearest-neighbor filtering on sprite/terrain atlases. Depth-of-field needs Sprite3D masked cutout / DopFix depth writes so the focus plane can resolve.
