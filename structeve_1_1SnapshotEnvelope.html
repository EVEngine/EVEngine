详细描述

Stable outer format shared by persistence and cross-process snapshots.

payload is owned by the envelope. schema identifies the payload contract, while schemaVersion identifies the version of that contract. Unknown payload fields remain the domain consumer's policy; unknown envelope fields are rejected by the strict parser.
