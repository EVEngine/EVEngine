详细描述

Real Box3D-backed, non-destructive simulation backend for scene placement.

The backend owns a private physics world and wrapper objects. Scene state is copied at admission and is never mutated by stepping. Selected objects are driven toward a shared handle while collision resolution remains authoritative inside the preview. The class is owner-thread-only and invokes no callbacks.
