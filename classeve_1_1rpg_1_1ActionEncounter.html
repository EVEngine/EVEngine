详细描述

Two-participant action encounter with generation-safe links and retryable kill delivery. @ownership Host owns this coordinator; ECS owns actors, GameState owns reward facts. @lifetime Retains handles and identity values only. Destroying either actor invalidates attacks. Recreate and bind after restore; no transient combat state is serialized. @thread Simulation owner thread only. @reentrancy Native provider supports queries must not mutate participants or registrations; no scripts are invoked by this coordinator.

返回

备注
Host owns range, cooldown and windup admission. Tick is injected and nondecreasing; no random stream is used. Pending player kill receipts block further hits until claimed.
