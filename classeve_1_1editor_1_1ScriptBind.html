详细描述

Editor facade binder: common script::BindContext plus history/owned-create helpers.

Use this instead of copying bindingFailure / null-self / owned-create stamps into every *EditorScriptBindings.cpp. Public addFunc names and arities stay unchanged. Non-editor domains should prefer eve::script::BindContext directly.
