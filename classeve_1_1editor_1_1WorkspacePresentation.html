详细描述

Shared validation and draft/commit controller used by domain editor UI adapters. @ownership Owns only a temporary draft. Never owns a document, selection or undo stack. @lifetime One controller belongs to one domain instance; cancel before switching sources. @thread Owner thread only. No callbacks or retained source pointers. @reentrancy Non-reentrant; host dispatches sequentially and closes at a safe point.
