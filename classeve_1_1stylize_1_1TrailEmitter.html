详细描述

Deterministic CPU sampler and ribbon mesh generator for weapon trails.

Call update(dt) once per simulation step, then append(root, tip). A large endpoint jump starts a disconnected segment, preventing teleport triangles. The emitter owns all samples and returned snapshots own their data. It is main-thread affine, performs no rendering, and invokes no callbacks.
