详细描述

Bounded provenance builder for explicit-coordinate baked recordings. @ownership Owns names and content digests; never retains source bytes or world pointers. @thread Owner-thread mutation; const encoding is reentrant without concurrent mutation. No callbacks or IO. Schemas EVECREC1 (metres) and EVEBREC1 (planar pixels) fix units, field order and length framing. Unknown versions/fields are rejected. Producers must supply algorithm/backend revisions, all source dependencies, parameters, the actual fixed timestep and sampling stride, and every named RNG seed. These describe admission, not cross-backend determinism.
