Atomically grant loot, notify quest progress, and consume one world object.

参数
gameStateBorrowed owner of attributes and persistent world facts.
trackerBorrowed quest-progress owner.
bagBorrowed inventory owner.
requestValidated interaction description; omitted effects use empty id and zero amount.
返回
Number of committed effects, or a structured failure with every owner unchanged.
备注
A required quest must be active. The object must not already be consumed. Inventory hooks run only after attribute, quest, world and inventory state are final. @ownership All participants remain caller-owned and must outlive this call. @thread Call on the participants' owning simulation thread; concurrent mutation is unsupported. @reentrancy Inventory hooks may re-enter only after the no-fail commit boundary.
