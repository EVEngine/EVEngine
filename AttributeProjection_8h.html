详细描述

Owner-aware canonical attribute state shared by gameplay adapters.

The projection owns only the selected gameplay numbers. Domain components remain the owners of movement, physics, animation, inventory and other non-attribute state. Legacy fields may be refreshed from this projection, but are never read back after initialization.
