class ArtifactStoreStage
 

详细描述

In-memory owner and publisher for generated artifacts.

The store owns records after publish. Pointers returned by find are borrowed and are invalidated by publish, remove or clear. Publishing never replaces an existing identity; build keys are lookup metadata and do not become identities.
