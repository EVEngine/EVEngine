详细描述

Workspace controller for a MagicaVoxel-style voxel sculpt project.

Owns the catalog of bounded occupancy models, local undo, and sculpt tools. The presenter renders with eve.Voxel() and picks with a camera ray.

@ownership Editor owns the document. Screen getters copy values. @threadaffinity Owner thread only. @reentrancy No unknown callbacks.
