详细描述

EvaluationContext backed by one borrowed RPG actor and SkillDefinition.

The actor and definition are observed only for the synchronous call to Condition::evaluate; this context does not own or retain them beyond that call. Attributes and resource amounts are read from the canonical AttributeSet. Tags include both skill-definition tags and active RPG status tags.
