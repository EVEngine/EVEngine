详细描述

Single mutable owner of imported terrain tiles and their complete-channel history. Caller-thread affine, synchronous, callback-free. Imported tiles are owned; mesh inputs are borrowed until return. Returned tile values own storage. Same-build deterministic floating arithmetic, no clocks or RNG. History publishes once after every tile succeeds. Snapshot schema procgen.road.terrain version 2 requires originY; version 1 migrates missing originY to zero. Unknown object fields are ignored; unsupported versions and malformed known fields reject atomically. Restore replaces current data and undo/redo history atomically; no external tile pointer or receipt survives.
