详细描述

Deterministic pooled runtime for AttackVfxRecipe instances.

@ownership Owns live instance slots and registered recipe copies. Layer GPU resources are owned by IAttackVfxLayerExecutor implementations. @thread Simulation-thread affine. No internal synchronization. @reentrancy Does not invoke user callbacks; may call registered executors.
