详细描述

Volumetric particle soft body using overlapping shape-matching clusters.

Particles form a regular 3D lattice in meter space (+Y up). Every lattice cell is an overlapping eight-particle cluster, which preserves local volume and orientation while allowing elastic and optional plastic deformation. The object is simulation-thread affine and owns all particle/constraint state. A World3D supplied through setCollideWorld() is borrowed and must outlive this object or be cleared before world destruction.
