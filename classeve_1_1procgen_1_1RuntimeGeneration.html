详细描述

View-driven partition and hierarchical procedural generation scheduler.

updateSource computes desired cells for every configured level. nextGenerate and nextCleanup provide bounded work queues; completeGeneration atomically publishes a cell output. The scheduler owns no scene or graphics objects.

void eve::procgen::RuntimeGeneration::setRefreshWorkBudget ( int  candidateCells)

Limit source-refresh planning work performed by one call.

参数
candidateCellsBounding-grid candidates examined per call; zero preserves synchronous refresh.

A positive budget stages desired cells without changing published queues until one complete source snapshot commits. Source changes arriving during a plan are coalesced into a later snapshot instead of restarting it.
