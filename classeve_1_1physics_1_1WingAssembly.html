详细描述

Immutable multi-surface aerodynamics, producing a single external-force batch. @ownership Copies geometry/polar values; stores no world/body or control-input pointers. @thread Build with immutable inputs on any thread; prepare on simulation owner outside callbacks. @reentrancy No callbacks, scripts or locks; failures never change bodies or partially apply surfaces.

Each surface samples actual body point velocity including omega cross r, minus uniform world wind. Controls rotate chord toward normal by the corresponding angle (radians), around the local span axis. Values are held for this prepared step; commit before moving bodies or changing controls/wind. This section model does not simulate wake interaction, downwash between surfaces, or CFD.
