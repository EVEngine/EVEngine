详细描述

Host-neutral adapter joining shop, quest-reward and world-loot transactions.

GameState, Tracker and Bag remain independent authoritative owners. This caller-owned adapter retains only borrowed pointers, an optimistic observation fingerprint and owning event projections. Destroy it before any participant.
