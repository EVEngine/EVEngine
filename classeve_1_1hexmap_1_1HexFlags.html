详细描述

Packed boolean cell state (roads, rivers, walls, exploration).

Bits 0-5 are roads per direction, 6-11 incoming rivers, 12-17 outgoing rivers, 18 walled, 20 explored and 21 explorable. Direction bit order matches HexDirection, so the direction helpers are plain shifts.
