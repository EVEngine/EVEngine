详细描述

Owning, pointer-free spline-to-transform binder with atomic document refresh.

The binder owns stable link identities but neither the spline document nor transform source. Callers pass both to each operation, so destroying either first cannot leave a retained pointer. Missing sources produce StaleHandle without changing the document or discarding the link, allowing the same binding snapshot to rebuild after scene reload.
