详细描述

Canonical house composition, blueprint and persistence operations. @thread World/registry owner-thread only. Inputs are synchronous borrows, never retained. @reentrancy Validation rules must be pure; committed events observe complete world state. @ownership Worlds own members and assembly records; all results own their payloads.
