详细描述

Bounded owning builder for fixed-topology deformation recordings. @ownership Owns a frozen recipe copy and accepted value frames, never a source/VM/world pointer. @thread Exclusive owner-thread mutation; no callbacks, IO, clocks or solver calls.

备注
Move-only. Moved-from instances may only be destroyed or assigned.
