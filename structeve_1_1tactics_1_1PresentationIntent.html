详细描述

One presentation instruction: what to show, where, and until when.

备注
Presentation is not authoritative and must be droppable: revision lets a consumer discard intents older than the state it is drawing, and expiresAtTick bounds every transient one. Geometry that the event stream does not carry (from, to, path) is filled from the accepted command the event points at, so there is still exactly one authority for it.
