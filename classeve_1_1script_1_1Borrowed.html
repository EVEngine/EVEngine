template<class T>
class eve::script::Borrowed< T >

Non-owning C++ observation with no implicit lifetime extension.

模板参数
TObserved object type, possibly const-qualified.
备注
The owner must outlive every use. This type intentionally has no reset-to-own or heap-management operation.
