const std::function< float(int, int)> &  entryPenalty

A* with a caller-owned non-negative dynamic entry penalty.

参数
sxStart cell x.
syStart cell y.
gxGoal cell x.
gyGoal cell y.
entryPenaltyAdditional cost for entering a walkable cell. Negative values are clamped to zero; non-finite values reject that cell.
返回
Caller-owned path, empty when no route satisfies the overlay. @ownership Ownership transfers to the caller, which must release the Path after its final synchronous use. @lifetime The returned Path remains valid until the caller releases it and does not borrow Pathfinder state.
