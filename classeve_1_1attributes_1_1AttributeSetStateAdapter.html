详细描述

Adapts one AttributeSet to numeric world reads and volatile writes.

Reputation is represented by an ordinary canonical attribute name (for example reputation.factionA); no dialogue-owned reputation mirror is introduced. Persistent changes must be sent to a StatePatch participant and later projected into the authoritative AttributeSet by its owning system.
