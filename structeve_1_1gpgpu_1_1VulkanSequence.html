详细描述

Vulkan implementation of Sequence: one command buffer per begin()/submit() cycle (allocated from the compute/upload pool, matching executeImmediately), with a persistent pool of host-visible staging buffers for recordUpload(). submit() ends the command buffer, submits once with a fence and waits.
