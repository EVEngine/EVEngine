详细描述

Process-owned state machine and evidence ledger for Agent game development.

All methods have main-thread affinity and never invoke callbacks. The singleton owns all strings and snapshots until reset or process shutdown. It does not own game, Editor, renderer, filesystem, or test-runner state; evidence is an immutable receipt that refers to those authoritative systems.
