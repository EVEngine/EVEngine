详细描述

Prepare CPU reference vertices from the same water view used by rigid bodies. Borrows mesh and water until build returns. They and the view's owners must remain alive and unmodified; do not queue this builder asynchronously or retain across simulation steps. No graphics, physics or resource pointer is retained in the published frame.
