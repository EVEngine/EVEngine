Voxelize a closed triangle mesh.

Unsigned distance is swept triangle-by-triangle over each triangle's expanded AABB; the sign comes from an even-odd raycast along +X.

参数
positionstriangle vertices, 3 floats each.
indicestriangle indices, 3 ints per triangle.
triangleCountnumber of triangles.
dimsvoxel resolution.
返回
signed field over the mesh's bounding box (padded one voxel).
