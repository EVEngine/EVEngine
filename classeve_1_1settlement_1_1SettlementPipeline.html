详细描述

Deterministically ordered, composable settlement pipeline.

The constructor installs the eight canonical stages. Additional stages can be inserted into any phase with a stable priority and unique name. Apply, Event and Trigger each have an unregistrable terminal step; custom stages in those phases always run before their terminal step regardless of priority or name. Other ordering is phase, priority, name, then registration sequence.
