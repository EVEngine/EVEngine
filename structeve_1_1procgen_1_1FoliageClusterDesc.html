详细描述

Recipe for one stylized leaf cluster ("foliage card bundle").

A cluster is a spherical volume filled with a handful of leaf planes. Each plane is a disk whose leaf centres come from a blue-noise (Poisson-disk) sampler, so the leaves stay evenly spread instead of clumping the way an independent per-leaf random draw does. Only leaf quads are emitted, which is the geometric equivalent of "leaves opaque, everything else transparent": the gaps between leaves cost no geometry, no blending and no sort order.

The planes are placed at stratified yaw angles around the cluster's Y axis with a small random tilt and offset, and vertex normals are transferred from the cluster sphere rather than taken from the leaf's own plane, so a bundle of flat cards still shades like a rounded mass of foliage.
