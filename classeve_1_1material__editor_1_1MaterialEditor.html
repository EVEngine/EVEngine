详细描述

Owned, UI-neutral material document with atomic patches and undo/redo. @ownership Owns the document, authority and transaction history; snapshots are detached values. @threadaffinity Owner thread only. No callbacks are invoked by this facade. @reentrancy Calls must not overlap. No runtime object pointers are retained.
