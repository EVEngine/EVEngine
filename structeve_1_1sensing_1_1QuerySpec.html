详细描述

Configurable candidate query against SensingWorld.

This is the single filtering/sorting implementation surface. Script circle/box helpers and Targeting adapters compose a QuerySpec rather than reimplementing acceptance rules. Domain/LOS/zone stay outside this World2D fact query; adapters that need them must inject projection/LOS capabilities explicitly.
