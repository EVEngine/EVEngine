详细描述

Optional scene-owned search capability; never owns or mutates scene entities. @ownership Process-lifetime provider; all inputs borrowed only during the call, outputs own data. @thread Engine thread at a safe point; no concurrent scene mutation. @reentrancy Calls no scripts, link callbacks or embedding services. Not reentrant.
