详细描述

Owner-thread 3D character authority for action combat.

Horizontal intent, jump, dodge i-frames and optional root-motion deltas are owned here. Ground is the plane y = 0 unless an ICombatGroundProvider is borrowed; capsule collisions are optional via ICombatMoveProbe. Projects project the resulting pose into Scene/Physics after each advance.
