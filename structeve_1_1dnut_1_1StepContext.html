详细描述

Borrowed execution context handed to one step handler.

Every member is borrowed for the duration of the synchronous handler call and must not be retained. Handlers run on the runtime's owner thread and must not re-enter the runtime that dispatched them.

◆ host

void* eve::dnut::StepContext::host = nullptr

Opaque consumer context installed by the runtime owner.

The language core never reads this pointer. A consumer that registers handlers uses it to reach its own per-run binding without making the core depend on that consumer's types. Its lifetime belongs to whoever called SequenceRuntime::setHostContext.
