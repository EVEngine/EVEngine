EVENGINE_API_FOUNDATION Result< ConvexHull3D > eve::math::convexHull3D ( std::span< const HullPoint3 >  points,
double  tolerance = 1e-10 
)

Incremental 3D hull with outward triangles and compact vertex indices.

参数
pointsFinite XYZ values, borrowed only during this call.
toleranceDistance relative to the largest bounding-box extent; finite, nonnegative and less than one. Coordinates are normalized internally.
返回
Owned hull or InvalidArgument. Empty/point/segment have no faces. Plane vertices form a boundary cycle with a triangle fan (orientation is unspecified). Volume faces retain coplanar triangulation; edge-interior vertices may remain. @ownership Result owns all output; input is neither changed nor retained. @thread Stateless, callable on any thread with immutable input; invokes no callbacks. @cost Worst-case O(n^2 log n) time and O(n) memory per point set; cache results for reuse.
注解
Sorted processing is deterministic for identical input sets on one platform. Floating-point predicates are approximate; near-degenerate features within tolerance may be omitted. No exact topology or cross-platform bitwise parity is promised.

inline

C1 polynomial maximum for blending two height fields.

参数
aFinite first height.
bFinite second height, in the same coordinate system and units.
widthFinite nonnegative height-difference band; zero selects ordinary max.
返回
max(a,b) outside the band, otherwise max(a,b) + width*h*h/4, where h = 1-abs(a-b)/width. Equal heights rise by width/4. Overflow returns +infinity. @thread Stateless and thread-safe; retains nothing and invokes no callbacks.
注解
Constant work per sample, no allocations or RNG. Symmetric but not associative. Results are deterministic on one toolchain; compare across platforms with tolerance. Preconditions are checked with EV_PARAM_CHECK in assertion-enabled builds.
