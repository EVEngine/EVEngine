详细描述

Atomic preflight/prepare/commit coordinator for one or two adapters.

备注
A same-container transfer creates one participant and one staged replacement. No post-commit snapshot or fallible restore is used.

Preflight and atomically transfer one object.

备注
No adapter is mutated or event emitted until every check and participant preparation passes. Same-container moves are supported as one participant. A callback warning does not make an already committed transfer appear to have failed.
