详细描述

Multi-document Montage workspace backed by the engine document service.

This owner-thread-only coordinator owns tab editors and one shared deep-copy clipboard. The injected DocumentService remains the persistence authority and must outlive the workspace. Each save publishes the editor's canonical target snapshot to DocumentService before requesting its revision-qualified CAS save.
