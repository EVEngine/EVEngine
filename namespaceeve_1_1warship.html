详细描述

Warship module entry (eve.Warship): hull/function catalogues, spawn, compartment remodel, helm orders, and frame update.

Warship domain data: hull templates, compartment functions, and the WarshipEntity short root.

Design note: docs/dev/通用战舰系统设计.md

Analogous to vehicle, but the distinctive surface is a huge hull whose internal compartment grid can be rearranged. Occupied cells project thrust, power, hangar, weapons, crew, and sail capabilities. Behavior lives in WarshipSystem; this header holds pure data.
