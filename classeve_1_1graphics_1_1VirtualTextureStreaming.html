详细描述

Internal UE5-style runtime virtual texture session: feedback → decode → atomic publish.

Owned by Material (enableVirtualTexture / addVirtualTextureLayer). Game scripts do not construct or tick this type; Material::bind advances residency once per Graphics::mesh3dDrawEpoch(). GPU resources stay in Graphics; this object only stages CPU pixels and generation-qualified upload batches.

@thread Not thread-safe; record feedback, update, and publish on one authoring/render thread. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks.
