详细描述

Owning lazy GPU resource resolver for texture-backed terrain Detail prototypes. @ownership Uploaded meshes and images are owned by the bound Graphics backend and leased by this resolver. @lifetime The reader and Graphics backend must outlive this object and its explicit release. @thread Construction, resolve, release, and destruction are graphics-thread affine. @reentrancy Calls no scripts or caller callbacks and must not be entered recursively.
