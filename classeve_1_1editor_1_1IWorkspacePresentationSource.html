详细描述

Synchronous adapter for an open domain editor, without UI or domain includes. @ownership Caller owns the source and keeps it alive for each controller call. @thread Owner thread only. No source reference is retained by the controller. @reentrancy Implementations must not re-enter the controller during a call.
