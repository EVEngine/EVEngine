详细描述

Bounded owning recorder of stable-identity rigid frames, without simulation dependencies. @ownership Owns recipe and accepted values; never retains body/world/VM pointers or runtime handles. @thread Exclusive owner-thread mutation; no callbacks, implicit clock reads or stepping.

备注
Move-only. Moved-from instances may only be destroyed or assigned.
