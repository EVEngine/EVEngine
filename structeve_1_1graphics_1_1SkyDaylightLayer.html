详细描述

Immutable clear-sky daylight material controls, copied during sky preparation.

Source RGB scattering keys are linear [angleDegrees,r,g,b], with constant endpoints. All colors are linear HDR. Orbit is [pitchDegrees,yawDegrees,verticalOffset,phaseTurns]. This is an upload value, not a clock, mutable authority or persistent schema. @thread Validation is pure and worker-safe; no callbacks or retained references.
