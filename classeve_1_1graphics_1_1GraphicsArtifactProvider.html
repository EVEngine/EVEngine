详细描述

Real graphics-module provider for generated mesh publication.

The provider always owns the backend-neutral descriptor. When a live Graphics backend is bound, prepare also calls its normal newMeshFromArrays upload boundary; an unavailable/uninitialized backend is an explicit observable CPU capability-degraded path rather than a hidden mock.
