◆ ObjectSemantic

enum class eve::script::ObjectSemantic
strong

The only three meanings a script-facing object may have.

Value is a detached canonical eve::Value tree. Owned is rooted or otherwise responsible for destruction. Borrowed is an observation whose lifetime is bounded by its owner and which must never be retained across a mutation, frame, task, callback, restore, or module unload.
