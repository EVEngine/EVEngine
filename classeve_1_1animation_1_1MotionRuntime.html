详细描述

Owns typed motion slots and applies SimulationStep updates.

@ownership Animation owns the runtime. Sinks and callbacks are borrowed. @thread Main-thread animation pump only; not synchronized. @reentrancy Callbacks must not spawn/cancel motions on this same runtime during advance (Phase 1 has no deferred command queue).
