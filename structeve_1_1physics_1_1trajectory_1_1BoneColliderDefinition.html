详细描述

Catalog entry that binds a sphere or capsule to named attachment points.

Sphere samples boneName once with localOffset. Capsule either:

  • samples the same bone twice with localOffset ± (0, halfHeight, 0) when endBoneName is empty (Physics Asset style), or
  • samples boneName/endBoneName with their local offsets (weapon-blade / dual-socket sweep style, matching UE5 anim-notify traces).

Filter bits mirror World3D query category/mask; they are applied only for the duration of each sweep and do not mutate persistent world defaults.
