详细描述

Composes Dialogue accounts with StatePatch or Action participants.

This adapter owns only copied payment configuration and temporary reservation participants. It owns no account balance, world state, action execution, callback, or provider. Participants are prepared in account order and effects are committed after account debits; if an effect fails, already committed debits are compensated. The call is synchronous and non-reentrant on the owner thread.
