详细描述

In-memory versioned replay evidence, independent of learned model state.

备注
Version 1 is the initial format. Replay rejects unknown schema/version; the canonical Value/JSON codec rejects unknown fields. Adapters retain their domain version separately and must reject incompatible domain builds. No older released schema exists; future migrations must be explicit.
