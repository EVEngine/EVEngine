详细描述

Authoritative architectural space document (Pascal-like node graph).

@ownership The document exclusively owns its nodes. Borrowed pointers returned by find() are invalidated by any mutating call. @thread Owner thread only. @failure Mutators return a checked Result and leave the previous document state unchanged.
