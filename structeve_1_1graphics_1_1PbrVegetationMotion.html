详细描述

Borrowed TVE Motion atlas/noise and owning explicit object-motion controls. Object mode requires an unskinned Mesh nine-float vegetation-deformation stream. Motion sampling uses the authored pivot, while repeat-addressed noise varies over each vertex's world position. Time and all controls are caller-owned snapshots; the renderer reads no clock or mutable global shader state.
