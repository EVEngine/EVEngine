详细描述

Ordered atomic receiver with duplicate suppression and bounded out-of-order buffering.

Parts never mutate PixelWorld individually. A complete next transfer is canonicalized and committed through applyChunkBatch; only then does the cumulative ACK advance. Failure leaves both replica and expected transfer id unchanged.
