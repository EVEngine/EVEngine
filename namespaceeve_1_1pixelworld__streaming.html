Verify and decode a version 1, 2 or 3 Chunk-batch archive into an owning candidate.

备注
Decoding is bounded and has no world side effects. Version 1 migrates with sourceLastEditSequence == 0; versions 1 and 2 migrate with fullResync == false. Bad envelope/Chunk hashes fail before any authority write.
