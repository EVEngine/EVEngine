详细描述

Combines a borrowed base pose source with ordered override/additive layers.

Pose sources such as AnimPlayer, AnimGraph, AnimStateMachine, or a nested mixer, plus masks/clips/skeleton, are referenced but not owned. The mixer owns the evaluation boundary: call advance once per tick and do not advance attached sources separately.
