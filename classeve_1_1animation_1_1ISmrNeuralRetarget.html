详细描述

Optional MeshRet/SMR neural retarget provider (implemented by animation_tensor).

Animation stays free of tensor includes; the L6 satellite registers this capability. When absent or not ready, classical two-bone IK SMR remains the active path.

@ownership Provider is owned by AnimationTensor; revoked before destruction. @thread Composition/owner thread only. No callbacks, script re-entry, or background work.
