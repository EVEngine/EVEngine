Return a canonical modifier in deterministic sequence order.

返回
A nullable borrowed pointer into the canonical AttributeSet, or nullptr when index is outside the current range. @ownership Borrowed; AttributeComponent owns the underlying modifier and the caller must not delete or transfer the pointer. @nullable Yes, for an invalid index. @lifetime Valid until the next component mutation, restore or destruction; copy the value or query again after that boundary. @thread Owner-thread-affine; no synchronization is provided. @reentrancy Side-effect free and invokes no external callbacks.
