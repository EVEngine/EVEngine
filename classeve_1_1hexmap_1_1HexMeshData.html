详细描述

CPU-side triangle soup for one hex chunk surface.

Vertices are intentionally not shared between triangles: the reference hex-map mesh builder emits every triangle with its own three vertices, which keeps the blend/terrace/cliff seams crisp and makes flat face normals exact. finalize() therefore computes per-face normals and duplicates them across the three vertices of each triangle.

注解
Owns plain host memory; upload is the caller's responsibility (gfx.newMeshFromArrays / gfx.updateMeshVertices).
