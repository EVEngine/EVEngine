const SkyAtmosphereLuts &  tables,

Integrate distant-sky ambient radiance per unit incident RGB irradiance at six kilometres.

Uses 64 fixed stratified directions, ten samples per ray, isotropic phase and no ground bounce. The caller must supply tables baked from the same immutable parameters. Direction is Y-up towards the light.

返回
Linear RGB radiance, or a structured invalid-input/numerical failure. @thread CPU-only; no global RNG, provider access or retained references. @cost Hundreds of medium evaluations; run during lighting preparation, never per pixel or draw.
