详细描述

Own renderer entities and their prepared prefab resources, without owning a Scene node. @ownership The ECS table owns entity storage; this adapter owns entity lifecycles and the preparation lease. @lifetime The ECS table, Graphics and resource factories must outlive this object. Unlink Scene links before release. @thread Graphics/ECS-owner thread only, outside view iteration for creation; no scripts, callbacks or locks.

备注
Runtime handles detect external entity deletion/reuse. Reimport creates a separate candidate before swap.
