详细描述

Canonical selected-attribute storage with owner and revision checks.

The class is synchronous and thread-affine to its owning ECS world. It owns the AttributeSet; callers do not retain pointers into it across structural ECS changes. restore first builds a candidate set and publishes it only after all validation and modifier insertion succeeds.
