◆ prepareGltfImport()

Result< PreparedAssetImport > eve::asset_import::prepareGltfImport ( const GltfImportRequest &  request)

Decode triangle primitives from glTF 2.0 into canonical typed mesh blobs.

返回
Owning candidate with meshes and, when present, PBR materials/images, skeleton, skin bindings and clips. @thread Worker-safe. @reentrancy Does not execute extensions, scripts, callbacks, filesystem or network access.
备注
v1 accepts float POSITION/NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0 and unsigned scalar indices; Unsupported primitive modes, sparse/compressed accessors, matrix skeletal nodes, non-LINEAR skeletal channels and morph animation fail explicitly. All contiguous weight sets (FLOAT or normalized unsigned bytes/shorts) are retained. Skeletal data stays in glTF metre/right-handed coordinates; ancestor nodes are retained in parent-before-child order.
