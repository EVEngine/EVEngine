详细描述

Incremental six-face HDR scene capture for a runtime reflection probe.

Captured faces remain linear RGBA16F Canvas resources. Publishing them as a filtered cubemap is a separate backend operation, so capture completion never exposes an unfiltered or partially updated probe to lighting.
