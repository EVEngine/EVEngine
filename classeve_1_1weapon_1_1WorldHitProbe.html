详细描述

Explicit sphere-query adapter; never registered or installed in a carrier runtime automatically. @ownership Owns its query session and optional mapping policy, but neither simulation world. @thread Simulation owner only; callbacks must not reenter or mutate the simulations.

备注
A missing session reports Unsupported. World destruction/restore is detected by the session. Destroying the probe first leaves physics intact. Recreate the session and gameplay links after restore.

Project physical contacts without applying damage or forces. @cost One sweep per proposed segment plus one mapper call per contact; amortized by simulation substeps.

返回
Owning contacts with physical provenance; provider/mapping failure publishes no contacts. Exceptions from the mapper become Failed diagnostics, so carrier updates retain frame atomicity.
