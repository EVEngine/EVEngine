详细描述

Data-driven biome distribution rules compatible with PointGraph and scene batches.

Candidates are sampled from a caller-provided domain, rejected by exclusion domains, assigned to the highest-priority matching layer, then deterministically select a weighted asset and transform from their independent point seed.
