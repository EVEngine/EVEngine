详细描述

Presentation bridge for a borrowed geometry-collection instance.

Fractured collections use prepared exterior/cut-face mesh batches. Legacy box collections keep per-bone proxies and revision-based sleeping batches. Animation submission copies poses into renderer-owned scratch before the engine forward-pass callback; graphics must outlive the renderer. Current submitted meshes also fill the same-frame GBuffer for fog occlusion. One process-lifetime dispatcher holds weak registrations; renderer teardown expires its registration before releasing meshes. Render-thread only, no reentrant teardown.

@ownership Never owns Instance, Graphics, or Body3D. Mesh caches are produced through Graphics and not retained as exclusive ownership. @lifetime Borrows Instance and Graphics only for each call; do not call draw after Instance or its World3D is destroyed.
