详细描述

Composed 2D mechanical assembly owned by a World.

Builds crank-slider, shaft, and ratchet mechanisms from Joint2D primitives. Ratchet one-way locking is applied automatically during World::step.

@ownership World owns the mechanism and the joints it creates. Borrowed Body inputs are never retained as caller ownership. @lifetime Valid until Mechanism2D::destroy(), World::destroy(), or dependent body/joint invalidation. @thread Call on the owning physics thread. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks.
