# Photographic preview environments

These six-face 128×128 LDR PNG sets are copied without modification from the
engine's existing environment fixtures. Face order: +X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z
(`px`, `nx`, `py`, `ny`, `pz`, `nz`).

Sources: Poly Haven contributors, licensed under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `studio_small_09`: https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09
- `kloppenheim_06_puresky`: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky

The committed PNGs are tone-mapped derivatives, not HDR data. The repository's
`test/Textures/env/_convert_hdr_to_cubemap.py` records the original conversion.
