# Resource provenance

- `assets/garden/tree-{0,1,2}.{gltf,bin}` and `leaf-spray.png` are reused unchanged from `examples/modern-balcony/assets/garden`. Geometry was generated by EZ-Tree (Daniel Greenheck, MIT), with fixed presets/seeds in `examples/modern-balcony/garden/build-trees.mjs`; the procedural leaf texture was authored in that example. The MIT notice is preserved in `assets/garden/LICENSE-EZ-TREE.txt`.
- `assets/gravel-albedo.png` was created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this example. It is used as a repeating ground albedo, without image-based ripples or foam. The exact generation prompt is in `ART_DIRECTION.md`.
- `assets/reeds.png` and `reference.png` are the generated resources described in `ART_DIRECTION.md`.
- Surface shaders, wave packets, mesh arrangement and procedural material textures are repository-authored.
- `assets/character` is the unmodified male base character and AnimationLibrary from `examples/character-motion-lab/assets/quaternius` (Quaternius, CC0 1.0). Both original license notices are included. `character.nut` adapts that example's explicit 53-bone retarget mapping and conventional material setup; the Walk and Idle clips are imported and retargeted at runtime. No new model was downloaded.
