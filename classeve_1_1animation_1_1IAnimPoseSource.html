详细描述

Borrowed pose provider consumed by AnimLayerMixer.

Implementations are not owned by the mixer. Callers must keep the source alive for as long as it remains attached as a base or layer. advance must be invoked by the mixer (or an equivalent evaluation owner); do not also advance the same source with the same tick elsewhere.
