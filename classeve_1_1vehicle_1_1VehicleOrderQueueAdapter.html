详细描述

Maps VehicleOrder domain payloads onto an orders::CommandQueue.

The adapter owns its queue and domain payload map. The observation returned by current() is borrowed until the next mutating adapter call. The legacy Orders::queue/current fields are refreshed by syncCompatibility() and are a read-only compatibility projection for existing Vehicle consumers.
