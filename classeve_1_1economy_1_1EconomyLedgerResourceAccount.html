详细描述

Adapts one caller-owned EconomyLedger to IResourceAccount.

Resource ids map directly to EconomyLedger type ids. The protocol adapter gives credit/debit strict all-or-nothing semantics for multi-resource operations; the legacy EconomyLedger::credit API remains available for gather overflow accounting and may still accept a partial credit at its compatibility boundary. The ledger owns balance, income, expense and capacity facts; this adapter owns only reservation state.

The referenced ledger is borrowed and must outlive this adapter. Operations are synchronous and must be called on the owner thread.
