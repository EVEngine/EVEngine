详细描述

Presentation adapter contract for ArchSpace documents.

Implementations must treat the document as read-only authority and rebuild derived state on sync. Missing optional backends are expressed by not registering a projector, not by silent no-ops inside gameplay code.

@thread Owner thread only. @reentrancy Must not invoke unknown callbacks while holding projector locks.
