详细描述

Exposes server-probed climbing actions without accepting authored world targets.

Runtime, physics world and pose remain borrowed authoritative collaborators. They must outlive this owner-thread-affine adapter. Commands carry only player semantics; begin always re-probes the borrowed authoritative world.
