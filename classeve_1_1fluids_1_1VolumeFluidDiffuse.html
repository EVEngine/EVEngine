详细描述

Bounded, one-way fluid-advected secondary particle pool. @ownership Owns particles and capacity; no fluid/world pointers are retained. @thread Simulation-thread only, including snapshots; no concurrent calls. @reentrancy Invokes no callbacks. Advance borrows its fluid only for that call.

Fixed input/time ordering repeats on one build, with float tolerance across platforms. No RNG or automatic emission is used by this pool.

Advances once using current liquid/gas velocity, after the caller steps the fluid.

参数
fluidBorrowed field source; it is not advanced or retained.
secondsInjected duration in (0,1/30].
minimumNeighborsRemove particles below this count, in [0,1000000].

Lifetime expires before sampling. Surviving particles use explicit Euler advection and sampled velocity. No pressure, collision or rigid feedback is applied. Field-query budget/invalid output failure preserves the whole pool. Survivor and query-position scratch retain their peak capacity, so stable-size frames reuse all three source-sized working arrays.
