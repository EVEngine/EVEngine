详细描述

Owning gas presentation cache, independent of the source solver. @ownership Owns frames and depth scratch; borrows arguments synchronously only. @thread Single caller, no callbacks or concurrent mutation.

EVEDUST1 version 1, little endian float32; unknown versions/fields and trailing bytes rejected. Load validates into a candidate before replacing this clip. Playback restores a dedicated render-only fluid, never a gameplay solver. Fixed-step fluid bake is repeatable on the same build; saved playback has no simulation or RNG. Particle births/deaths use recorded frame boundaries.
