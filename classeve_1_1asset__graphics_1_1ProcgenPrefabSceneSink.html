详细描述

Scene-sink decorator projecting exact-build procedural prefab bindings into owned ECS renderers. @ownership Scene remains the author of batch metadata; cached snapshots are renderer projections guarded by its revision. @lifetime Graphics, underlying Scene sink, ECS table and admitted prefab factories outlive this object and release. @thread Graphics/Scene/ECS owner thread, outside iteration; synchronous, non-reentrant, no locks.

备注
Composition owns capability registration/unregistration and makes this the sole batch writer while installed. Unbound asset labels remain metadata anchors. No implicit IO, prefab lookup by label, Scene links or simulation System. Admitted mesh/image leases must belong to the bound Graphics device/context; cross-context variant admission is unsupported. Capacity bounds: 4096 admitted asset/build versions, 64 active/retired transaction batches, and 65536 peak instances/renderer parts across active, retired and prepared snapshots. Collect retired resources between large publications. Each delta collection is bounded to 65536 entries before staging.
