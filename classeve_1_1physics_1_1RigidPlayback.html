详细描述

Independent clock over a frozen rigid track; no solver, world, renderer or wall clock. @ownership Holds a cheap immutable track copy sharing sample storage, plus value-owned clock state. @thread Mutate each player exclusively; copies may run independently. No callbacks or IO.
