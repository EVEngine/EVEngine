详细描述

Native orchestration of Pcg RuntimeStamper's load, flatten, fit, mask, and stamp sequence.

The controller owns a copy of the loaded raster. execute() builds a complete terrain candidate and publishes it only on success. It retains no external pointer and invokes no callbacks. @thread Game thread only; callers must serialize access to the target heightmap.
