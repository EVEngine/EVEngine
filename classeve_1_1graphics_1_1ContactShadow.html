详细描述

Screen-space contact shadows for the primary directional light.

Ray-marches a short distance along the light direction through the GBuffer hardware depth attachment and darkens the already-lit scene with a black alpha overlay (same blending contract as AmbientOcclusion overlays).

@thread Render thread only.
