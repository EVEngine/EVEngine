详细描述

Render-thread-owned GPU projection of immutable spectral coefficients, with no independent clock. Vulkan compute reference parity is numerical, not bitwise. No callbacks during submission and no locks. Graphics retirement releases compute resources before the device; either destruction order is safe. A new submission invalidates all earlier tokens, including after a failed partially executed submission. Backend failure poisons the projection; rebuild it instead of appending commands to a failed recording.
