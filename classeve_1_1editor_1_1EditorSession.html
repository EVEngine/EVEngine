class EditorTargetCoordinator
 

详细描述

Hosts interchangeable editor tools and routes their lifecycle/input.

Tools are non-owning: the caller must keep a registered tool alive until it is removed or the session is destroyed. All methods are owner-thread only. Tool callbacks may re-enter the session; lifecycle state is detached before cancellation or deactivation callbacks are invoked.
