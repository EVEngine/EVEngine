Build the min/max hierarchy from a hardware depth texture.

参数
depthHardware depth source, near=0 and far=1.
maxLevelsMaximum hierarchy levels to build for the active quality budget.
返回
RG min/max depth atlas; level zero starts at x=0 and each lower level is packed directly to the right of its predecessor. @lifetime The returned texture is owned by this DepthPyramid until the next target resize.
