EVENGINE_API_FOUNDATION eve::Result< std::vector< std::string > > eve::emergence::collectWatchKeys ( const decision::Condition &  condition)

Collect the canonical fact keys a condition depends on.

Leaf nodes contribute their domain key. PolicyCall nodes contribute policy:<name> plus every declared scriptDeclaration.dependencies entry (already expected to be canonical fact keys). All/Any/Not recurse into children.

参数
conditionSide-effect-free condition tree.
返回
Sorted unique keys, or a structured failure when a PolicyCall lacks both a name and a dependency declaration that would make indexing safe. @thread Pure; reentrant; no callbacks.
