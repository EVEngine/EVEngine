详细描述

The charge rule a policy declares for round scheduling.

This is the one place a policy states how charge affects scheduling. The battle owns the mutation (per-unit charge lives in TurnResources, the single authority for per-unit turn state) and the ordering (via order plus the caller's canonical tie-break); the policy only declares the numbers. A policy therefore stays a stateless value-like object and schedules identically after a save/restore.

The default value — all zeros — means "no charge model": every living unit is ready every round and activating costs nothing, which is exactly the one-activation-per-unit-per-round behaviour of a pure ordering policy.
