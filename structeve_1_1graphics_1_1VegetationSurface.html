详细描述

Owning surface inputs, supplied afresh to avoid accumulating tint/wetness across frames. Color inputs are linear. Overlay, wetness, projection and occlusion controls are [0,1]. The fragment stage derives the final overlay mask from mesh variation and occlusion, the mapped normal and textured luminance. It then applies overlay color/normal/material response followed by wetness color/normal/smoothness.
