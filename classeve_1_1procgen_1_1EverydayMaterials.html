详细描述

CPU material factory with bounded, intuitive parameters. @ownership Returned sets uniquely own their maps. Inputs are borrowed only for the call. @thread Any thread with distinct inputs; no callbacks, registry access or retained pointers.

备注
Same seed and effective parameters give identical bytes on one toolchain; floating-point noise may differ across platforms. No simulation time or global RNG.
