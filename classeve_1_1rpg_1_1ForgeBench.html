详细描述

Owns a production WorkQueue and borrows bag / optional equipment+actor.

Craft pays bag materials and grants outputs. Upgrade mutates one bag instance's enhance prop. When equipment + actor are bound, successful upgrade/equip paths can refresh modifiers through EquipmentSystem.
