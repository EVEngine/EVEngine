inlineoverridevirtual

Queue guide AOV capture for one pack revision / shot set; never block on GPU.

参数
packJsonCanonical pack JSON borrowed for validation/copy on this call.
shotIdsEmpty means all shots in the pack.
返回
Opaque job token, or Unsupported / budget diagnostic. @cost Extra offscreen raster + readback per shot × AOV; reuse the resulting artifacts.
