详细描述

Deterministic Walk / Sprint / Crouch state machine.

Priority: Crouch > Sprint > Walk. Sprint requires grounded and not crouching. Crouch latches while !canStand so stand-up cannot teleport into geometry. Does not integrate motion or query physics — callers drive moveCapsule.
