详细描述

Physics module — Box2D (2D) + Box3D (3D) rigid bodies, plus interactive 2D/3D cloth (Verlet, self-collision, fold limit, body collision) and SPH fluid. Script: physics <- eve.Physics(); world <- physics.newWorld(0, 900); world3 <- physics.newWorld3D(0, -9.8, 0); cloth3 <- physics.newCloth3D(18, 12, 0.5, 0, 5, 0);
