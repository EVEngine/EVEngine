详细描述

Owner-thread deterministic damage coordinator.

apply prepares and validates the complete outcome before one assignment to target state. It uses no clock or RNG. IEEE-754 results are deterministic for identical inputs on supported backends; replay comparison uses exact stored doubles produced by this single resolution path.
