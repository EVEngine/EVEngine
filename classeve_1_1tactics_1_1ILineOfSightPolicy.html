详细描述

Answers "can @p from see @p to" for one board.

备注
Implementations must be pure and deterministic: the answer feeds replay, so it may not depend on a clock, a random draw or mutable state. Symmetry is a property of an implementation, not something the caller may assume.

Report whether from can see to.

返回
Whether sight is unobstructed, or a structured refusal for inputs the policy cannot answer (cells on different layers, or cells the board does not contain).
备注
Endpoints never block: a unit standing in smoke can still see out of its own cell, and a blocker on the target cell protects the target without hiding it.
