详细描述

Coordinates a resource debit with one domain effect participant.

The effect is prepared before the resource reservation is committed. A preparation failure therefore never charges the account. If a later commit fails after the effect became visible, Coordinator uses the participant's compensation contract to restore the effect and the account. This helper owns neither participant nor account; all references are borrowed for the synchronous call and must remain valid until it returns. The caller and both participants must use the same owner thread.
