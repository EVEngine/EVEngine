详细描述

Vulkan-resident multi-sheet 3D XPBD cloth with explicit backend errors. @ownership Owns buffers, compute shader and sequence. Graphics owns the draw mesh. @lifetime Borrowed Gpgpu and Graphics/device must outlive this object, including destruction. Destroy only after the last draw frame is presented, never inside an open pass. @thread Render/submission thread only. Call update before render-pass recording. @reentrancy No callbacks; update/draw/diagnostics must not overlap. Grid topology only; tolerance-bounded swept point-face/edge-edge contacts, no rigid-body coupling. GPU floating-point/contact-list ordering is tolerance-bounded, not bit-exact.
