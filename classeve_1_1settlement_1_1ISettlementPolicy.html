详细描述

Domain-owned policy hooks used by the common settlement pipeline.

Implementations must inspect state and prepare candidates only in the calculation stages. prepareTrigger must also be side-effect free; observable trigger work is represented by the committed event and can be consumed by the owning domain after settlement returns.
