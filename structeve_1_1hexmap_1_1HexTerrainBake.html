详细描述

Packed terrain produced by a generator and applied onto a hex map.

This is the consumer-owned contract: hexmap asks for cells, not for a generator algorithm. Planar bakes fill a rectangular offset grid; sphere bakes fill a Goldberg topology that must already exist on the live map.

注解
The packed cells string in the script Value uses little-endian HexValues then HexFlags (eight bytes per cell).
