详细描述

RTS domain roots and composition-only components.

RTS deliberately has several small ECS roots instead of a universal GameObject/GameplaySubject base. Unit, Building, Player and Faction own only their domain identity; orthogonal state is attached as components and cross-domain relationships are represented by typed links.
