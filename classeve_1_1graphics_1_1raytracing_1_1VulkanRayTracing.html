Trace mirror-style reflections into dest (RGBA16F, A = hit mask).

参数
gfxActive Graphics backend (Vulkan).
sceneColorLit scene color to sample on a hit when no material is bound.
hwDepthHardware depth (D32) matching the scene projection.
worldNormalWorld-space normals (RGB).
destOutput canvas; size drives the launch dimensions.
invViewProjInverse view-projection (RH + ZO) for primary rays.
viewProjView-projection used to reproject hit points into scene color.
eyeWorldCamera eye in world space.
返回
Unsupported when RT is unavailable; NoOp when the TLAS is empty; Failed on GPU errors. Callers must treat NoOp as "no usable output". @ownership All pointers are borrowed for the call; none are retained.
