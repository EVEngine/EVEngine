详细描述

Immutable fixed-topology deformation clip, independent of simulation and rendering providers. @ownership Shares frozen frames and a copied recipe; input mutation or source destruction cannot affect playback. @thread Const access is reentrant with independent output buffers. No callbacks, wall clock reads or solver calls.

备注
Vertex indices remain stable throughout the clip. Connectivity or UV changes require a new clip.
