详细描述

Bounded transient floating-number presentation owned by a game/view. @ownership Owns styles and all live popups; never owns gameplay, fonts or graphics. @thread Owner/render thread only. No callbacks, locks, wall clock or global RNG. @lifetime clear on scene teardown/reload. No persistent format or cross-frame object links. Equal requests and accumulated time give equal samples within 1e-4 pixel floating tolerance.
