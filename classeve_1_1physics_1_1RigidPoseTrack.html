详细描述

Frozen fixed-roster rigid poses, independent of physics, graphics and script providers. @ownership Copies share immutable owning data; prepare consumes values and retains no caller aliases. @thread Const operations and separate copies are reentrant. No callbacks, clock reads or IO during sampling. @lifetime Samples and identities survive destruction of the original recording and simulation world. Frames may arrive with different sample order, but missing, duplicate or new IDs are rejected. Position interpolation is linear; rotations use shortest-path normalized interpolation, not angular turns.

Result< void > eve::physics::RigidPoseTrack::save ( std::string_view  path) const

Publish an immutable track without replacing an existing asset. @cost Linear in samples plus filesystem barriers; save once outside playback.

返回
Checked publication status, with the same failure semantics as publishBakedAsset. EVERIGD1 v1 stores explicit assets without provenance; v2 embeds complete validated provenance. Both store coordinates, sorted persistent IDs, frame data and checksum. Cache admission requires loadForRecipe.
