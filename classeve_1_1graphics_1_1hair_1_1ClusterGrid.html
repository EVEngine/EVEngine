详细描述

Uniform grid over strand roots for CPU frustum culling.

Clusters are built from root positions hashed into cells of cellSize. Each cluster AABB covers all points of the curves assigned to that cell.

@thread Affine to the caller; not synchronized. @reentrancy Does not invoke callbacks.
