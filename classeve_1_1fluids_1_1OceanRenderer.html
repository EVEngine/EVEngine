详细描述

Graphics projection of an ocean; never owns or advances simulation state. Render-thread only, non-reentrant, no callbacks. Graphics owns GPU resources; a weak lifetime token makes provider-first destruction safe. Destroying this object first releases its resources. Do not retain frame builders across this renderer or water-owner mutation/destruction.
