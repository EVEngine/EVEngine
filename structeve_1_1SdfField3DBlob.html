详细描述

Portable dense SDF asset blob (schema id + version + samples).

Interchange between physics DistanceField3D and fluids MeshSdf. Outside the lattice, DistanceField3D uses constant outsideDistance; MeshSdf extends by Euclidean distance — consumers must document which boundary policy they apply after import.
