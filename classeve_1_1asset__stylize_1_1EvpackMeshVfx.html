详细描述

Owning package-backed mesh effect, using the canonical MeshVfxAsset playback and curves. @ownership Owns playback and shader leases. Graphics must outlive it; destroy before device shutdown. @lifetime Mesh/texture inputs are never retained. Load/reload copy package data; readers may be destroyed immediately. @thread Graphics-thread affine, no callbacks, locks or reentrancy. Time comes exclusively from advance(dt).
