详细描述

Tuning knobs of one fluid simulation.

Units are normalized (mass = 1 per particle, rest density 1). The support radius defaults to 4x the particle radius, the common PBF/SPH choice, so a particle sees roughly a 2-cell neighborhood in the uniform grid.
