详细描述

Compiled rig with an owned skeleton snapshot and indexed solve schedule. @ownership Owns all retained inputs. Returned pose/transform values are independent. @thread One owner thread per instance; independent instances may evaluate concurrently. @reentrancy No callbacks. Inputs are borrowed only for each synchronous call.

Controls use positive uniform scale to avoid unrepresentable shear. Compilation resolves names; evaluation uses indices. Time is supplied explicitly, clamped to duration.
