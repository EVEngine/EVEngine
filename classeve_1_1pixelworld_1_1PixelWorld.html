详细描述

Sparse, chunked, deterministic 2D falling-material world.

PixelWorld is the sole authority for material state. It is simulation-thread affine and invokes no callbacks. Rendering and physics consume snapshots or queries through adapters; neither owns a mutable copy of the world.
