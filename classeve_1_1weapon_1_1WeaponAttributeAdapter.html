详细描述

Adapts only Mana/Stamina trigger resources to AttributeProjection.

Ammo, charges, reload state, cooldown, spread, recoil and attack phase remain weapon-owned state. The legacy Resource fields are refreshed only as a one-way projection after canonical reads/writes.
