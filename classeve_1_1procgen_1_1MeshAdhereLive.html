详细描述

Real-time dynamic fusion session: source A sticks to surface B with adjustable params.

Owns copies of A/B baselines. Derived display mesh is recomputed on dirty evaluate. Bake is optional freeze; RemoveSetup restores the A baseline. Owner-thread only; no callbacks.
