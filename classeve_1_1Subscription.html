详细描述

Move-only RAII token that owns one observer registration.

Destroying or explicitly disposing the token cancels the registration. The cancellation operation is idempotent, and a moved-from token is inactive. The cancellation callback must be non-throwing; dispose() is noexcept and is therefore suitable for destructor use.

A token belongs to the thread-affine observer that created it. It must be disposed or destroyed on that observer's owner thread unless the observer explicitly documents a stronger cross-thread guarantee.
