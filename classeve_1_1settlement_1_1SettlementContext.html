详细描述

Mutable calculation frame visible to policy and custom stage code.

The frame owns no domain state. It exposes only the working amount and explanation accumulators; adapters keep health/armor/shield facts in their own authoritative store. After canonical Apply preparation, amount and numeric result inputs are frozen. After canonical Event preparation, stage details are frozen too. An attempted late mutation records a fatal pipeline violation instead of silently creating stale prepared data. All methods are synchronous and the frame must not escape a settle call.
