枚举值
None 

No reproduction.

PairGestation 

Two parents; carrier gestates then spawns offspring.

SelfBud 

Single mature parent; interval then spawn (alien budding).

Fission 

Single parent splits; optional parent death or stage reset.

SporeBurst 

Periodic offspring bursts without a partner.
