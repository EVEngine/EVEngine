详细描述

Module-based shape grammar expanded continuously along a 3D polyline.

Grammar syntax follows UE PCG's useful subset: comma-separated symbols, bracket groups, * (zero or more), + (one or more), and an integer repeat suffix. Module symbols do not contain decimal digits, which are reserved for exact repetition. Repetitions fill available spline length without crossing its end.
