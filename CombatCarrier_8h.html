详细描述

Composable combat-carrier runtime: motion ops, triggers, and impacts.

A carrier is a pooled flight vehicle. Ballistics (motionOps), firing timing (triggers), and attack outcomes (impacts) are assembled on a CarrierRecipe without hard-coding projectile subclasses. Damage settlement stays outside this module — impacts emit owning CarrierEvent values for game/combat adapters.
