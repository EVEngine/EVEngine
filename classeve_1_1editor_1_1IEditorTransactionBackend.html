详细描述

Transaction boundary consumed by editor property and other UI models.

Implementations are borrowed by the model and must outlive every synchronous call. The boundary owns the pending transaction and history; callers must inspect every structured result. Implementations are owner-thread-only and must not invoke an unknown callback while coordinating a transaction.
