详细描述

Strongly typed, transaction-friendly resource cost protocol.

The protocol deliberately owns no gameplay policy and no balance storage. An implementation may keep resources in an AttributeSet, an economy ledger, or another domain-specific store; the protocol only defines the atomic operation and reservation semantics at the boundary.
