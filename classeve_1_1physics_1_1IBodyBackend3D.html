详细描述

Solver-independent control of a rigid body, exclusively owned by its facade. @ownership Implementation owns the native body; parent invalidation detaches before world teardown. @thread Owning simulation thread only, between steps; no callbacks or reentrant mutation. @lifetime Results own their data. No borrowed native state escapes this interface.

备注
Failed commands return StaleHandle, InvalidArgument or Unsupported before mutation. Units are metres, seconds and kilograms; rotations use XYZW quaternions.
