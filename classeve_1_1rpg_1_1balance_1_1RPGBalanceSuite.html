详细描述

Configured build/strategy/scenario tournament using production RPG Battle and settlement. @ownership Owns validated suite definitions. Each measure owns a private ECS table and event scope. Reports own values; no actor pointer, callback, event or handle escapes a bout. @thread RPG owner thread, synchronous, configure/measure reentry forbidden. Registries/hooks must not be changed during a tournament; skill/effect/class definitions are the game's canonical registries.

备注
Version-1 suite uses instant turn-based skills; cast-time skills are explicitly rejected.
