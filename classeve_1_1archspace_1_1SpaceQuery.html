详细描述

Presentation-agnostic query facade over an ArchSpace document.

@ownership Borrows the document; does not own or mutate it. @lifetime Valid while the borrowed document remains alive and is not mutated. @thread Owner thread only. @cost Building a SpaceQuery is O(nodes). Individual queries are O(regions) or BFS over the portal graph unless noted.
