详细描述

A prepared consumer mutation with a non-throwing visibility boundary.

commit and rollback are called on the preparing thread. Implementations preallocate/copy everything in prepare; an unexpected allocation or broken invariant during commit is a process-fatal contract violation and must not be reported as a recoverable partial success.
